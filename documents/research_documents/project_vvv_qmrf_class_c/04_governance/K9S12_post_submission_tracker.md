### K9-S12: A Testable Prediction for Extended Wigner's Friend Experiments

**One-sentence summary:**
K9_E predicts a measurable deviation from Standard QM in an Extended Wigner's Friend
optical experiment — testable with a single quarter-wave plate (QWP) modification to
existing setups, with Figure of Merit FOM = 8.6 (detectable at N ≈ 91,000 coincidences).

**Quantitative predictions:**

| Observable | K9_E prediction | QM null | Significance |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|--------------|
| Genuine LF 1 | **+0.0891** | 0 | **8.6σ** above null |
| δ⟨A₁B₂⟩ | **−0.0355** | 0 | **20.8σ** above null |
| Figure of Merit | **FOM = 8.6** | — | Detectable at N ≈ 91,000 |

**Experimental setup (minimal modification to Bong et al. 2020):**

```
Protocol:  Modified Bong Protocol — K9-S12
Equipment: Single quarter-wave plate (QWP) before Superobserver measurement
Setting:   α = 31° (QWP angle, single setting)
Sample:    N ≈ 91,000 coincidences per setting
Loophole:  Loophole-open (screening test) — fair-sampling assumed
Reference: Bong et al. (2020) arXiv:1907.05607 (base protocol)
Pre-reg:   K9S12_PreRegistration_Protocol.md (binding for confirmatory status)
```

**Why this experiment matters:**
This is the FIRST dedicated test of K9_E's registration-layer suppression hypothesis.
Existing experiments (Proietti et al. 2019, Bong et al. 2020) cannot distinguish K9_E
from Standard QM due to noise sensitivity — a single QWP modification enables the test
with equipment already in place at optical EWF labs.
